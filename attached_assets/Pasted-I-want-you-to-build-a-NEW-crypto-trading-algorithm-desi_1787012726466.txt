# STEP 2 — TREND STRATEGY

When regime = TREND UP:

Look for LONG pullback opportunities.

Example logic:

1. Higher timeframe must NOT be bearish.
2. 1h structure bullish.
3. Price pulls back toward EMA20 or EMA50 on entry timeframe.
4. RSI cools into a reasonable pullback zone.
5. RSI then starts turning upward.
6. MACD/momentum begins improving.
7. Price confirms by closing back above the relevant short-term EMA or producing a bullish confirmation candle.

For TREND DOWN:

Reverse the logic for SHORT positions.

Do not chase heavily extended candles.

# STEP 3 — RANGE / BUY-LOW-SELL-HIGH STRATEGY

When regime = RANGE:

This strategy should actively seek mean-reversion opportunities.

LONG candidate:

1. Price moves into the lower part of its recent trading range OR reaches the lower Bollinger Band.
2. RSI becomes relatively oversold.
3. Higher timeframe is NOT strongly bearish.
4. Momentum stops deteriorating and begins recovering.
5. Confirmation occurs on a new candle.

Enter LONG.

Exit toward:

- Bollinger midline
- recent range midpoint
- or a predefined modest R target

Reverse for SHORT if appropriate.

This is the part of the algorithm intended to:

BUY LOW → SELL HIGH → REPEAT

But do not blindly buy every fall.

# STEP 4 — BREAKOUT STRATEGY

Detect price compression / consolidation.

LONG breakout candidate:

1. Price breaks above meaningful recent resistance.
2. Entry closes beyond the range rather than merely wicking through it.
3. Volume or volatility expansion supports the breakout.
4. 1h direction does not strongly oppose the breakout.
5. Avoid entries after abnormally oversized candles.

Reverse for SHORT breakouts.
