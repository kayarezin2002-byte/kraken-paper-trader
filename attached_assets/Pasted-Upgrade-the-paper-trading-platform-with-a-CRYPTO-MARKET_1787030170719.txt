## 8. Do not force trades

Increasing the number of cryptocurrencies is intended to increase the number of genuine opportunities available.

Do NOT lower standards just because more assets are being scanned.

Keep the initial ACTIVE threshold at:

4/6

The idea is:

more markets + same reasonable threshold = more opportunities

rather than:

fewer markets + extremely weak entry requirements.

if they do not meet the trading requirements.

## 18. Portfolio protection

Even though many cryptocurrencies are being scanned, do NOT allow unlimited simultaneous positions.

Make these configurable:

Maximum open crypto positions
Maximum total portfolio risk
Maximum risk per trade
Maximum exposure to highly correlated assets

For initial PAPER testing use sensible conservative limits.

Do not let 10 cryptocurrencies all take essentially the same BTC-driven market movement and accidentally multiply portfolio risk.

## 21. Performance requirements

Do NOT fetch huge amounts of candle data for every cryptocurrency on every page refresh.

Build this efficiently.

The scanner should use lightweight recent data for signal calculation.

Load deeper chart history only when the user actually opens an asset or requests an older chart range.

Cache market data where appropriate.

Do not allow adding more cryptocurrencies to make the mobile dashboard slow or unstable.
